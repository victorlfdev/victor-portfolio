# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: small business owners and marketing decision-makers seeking a freelance web developer. Secondary: technical leads evaluating a developer for collaboration. Situation: evaluating whether Victor can deliver polished, production-ready front-end work for their brand or product. Job: "Hire someone who ships real interfaces, not just prototypes."

## Product Purpose
Serve as the single point of truth for client acquisition and service offerings. Demonstrate technical capability through shipped work, communicate a product-minded approach to development, and convert visitors into inquiries. Success = a qualified conversation or project inquiry.

## Positioning
A developer whose audiovisual background and product mindset produce interfaces that don't just work—they persuade. Where others deliver pages, Victor delivers pages that convert.

## Operating Context
Single-page React SPA served as a static site, bilingual (Portuguese/English), dark/light theme. Viewers arrive from LinkedIn or GitHub links, scroll through sections sequentially, and click through to live projects or contact channels. No authentication, no backend — entirely static, entirely client-side.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Bilingual content (PT/EN) driven from a single content file. Six full-bleed sections with scroll-driven reveals. Smooth scrolling via Lenis. Projects display embedded media (images, video) and link to live GitHub repos. Contact section provides direct email, phone, and social links. No form submissions, no analytics, no CMS. Must remain fast, minimal, and entirely client-side.

## Brand Commitments
Voice: delivery-focused, product-minded, clear. Positioning: audiovisual background informing interface craft. Name: vlf. (as seen in navbar branding). Existing assets: profile photo, project screenshots/video, favicon, OG image.

## Evidence on Hand
Portfolio content: `src/content/portfolio-content.ts`. Profile photo: `src/assets/victor-profile.jpg`. Project media: `src/assets/project-*.png`. Brand assets: `public/favicon.svg`, `public/og-image.png`. Social links: LinkedIn, GitHub. No testimonials, case studies, or metrics exist — do not fabricate.

## Product Principles
1. **Shipped over theoretical.** Every piece of content must reference something that exists and runs.
2. **Product thinking before pixel-pushing.** Design decisions are justified by what they accomplish for the visitor, not by aesthetic trends.
3. **Clarity compounds.** Simple structure, readable copy, and honest presentation beat clever execution every time.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility expectations: proper heading hierarchy, meaningful alt text, keyboard navigation, sufficient color contrast. No specific WCAG level or accommodation requirements have been confirmed.
